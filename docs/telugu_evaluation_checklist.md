# Telugu Pronunciation & Naturalness Evaluation Checklist

This checklist is designed for human evaluation of the 5 generated Telugu test sentences produced by the FastSpeech 2 model. As a native or fluent Telugu speaker, you can evaluate each sample on the criteria below.

---

## Evaluation Criteria Scale (1 to 5)
- **1 (Unacceptable):** Unintelligible, completely distorted, wrong sounds.
- **2 (Poor):** Severely broken, mispronounced words, unnatural cadence.
- **3 (Fair):** Understandable with effort, noticeable pronunciation flaws or robotic pitch.
- **4 (Good):** Clear pronunciation, natural rhythm, minor prosody or acoustic artifacts.
- **5 (Excellent):** Studio-like naturalness, crisp akshara articulation, native Telugu prosody.
